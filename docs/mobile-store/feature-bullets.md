# VyGo — Feature Bullet Library

Benefits-first copy bank. Each bullet is 3–5 lines (banner caption + sub-bullets) usable in the
long description, a launch marketing sheet, or A/B-tested store bullets. All claims are grounded
in the shipped code (`frontend/src/pages/*`, `components/seat3d/*`, `docs/*`).

---

## P1 · Search (hero)

**Bullet:**
> Compare and book the full Sri Lanka bus network in one screen.

- Live departures across Colombo, Kandy, Galle, Matara, Jaffna, Trincomalee, Katunayake airport and more.
- One-tap corridor chips: E01 Southern Expressway, E03 Colombo–Katunayake, classic numbered routes (1, 2, 4, 99, 122, 138...).
- Filter by bus type — AC, Luxury, Semi Luxury, Normal — and switch to expressway-only with a single toggle.

---

## P2 — Live availability (trust)

**Bullet:**
> Real seat counts, not estimates. Every trip shows exactly what's left before you commit.

- Results show remaining seats per departure, so "have a booking" means "a seat already held for you."
- Fares in LKR per seat and sort by departure time or fare low→high.
- Departure and arrival times, route distance and bus registration on every trip card.

---

## P3 — 2D/3D seat map (differentiator)

**Bullet:**
> Pick your exact seat on a real bus — not a colour-coded grid on a map. VyGo renders the actual coach in 3D.

- Walk the aisle with 3D/2D seat maps of the literal vehicle you'll ride (GLB models such as Viking Yutong C12).
- Available / selected / locked / booked seats are colour-coded; select up to 6 seats at once.
- Switch 3D ⇢ 2D instantly; manual 2D on low-power phones keeps the flow fast.
- Locked seats are held while you pay, so no one steals your pick.

---

## P4 — Secure payment (PayHere)

**Bullet:**
> Checkout is a single "Pay now" — seats locked, card handled by PayHere, receipt instantly issued.

- Pay with card through Sri Lanka's PayHere gateway — no cash, no counter queue.
- Booking reference and transaction ID recorded the moment you confirm.
- Seats are held during payment so you never lose your row to a slower buyer.
- We never store full card details on the device.

---

## P5 — QR e-ticket (core mobile offer)

**Bullet:**
> Board with your phone. Your ticket is a QR the conductor scans at the door.

- Instantly generated QR tickets with seat number and booking reference.
- Show the QR at the gate; conductor verifies (see Conductor app) — no printed tickets.
- Every confirmed booking stores the QR in My Bookings and Booking Detail.
- Works offline-friendly: the ticket lives in your account, refreshed on open.

---

## P6 — Bookings, refunds & account

**Bullet:**
> One account for every journey: book, follow up, cancel and refund.

- My Bookings lists every trip with status (Upcoming, Confirmed, Completed, Cancelled).
- Cancel and claim refunds under the fare rules shown when you book.
- Booking details show boarding/drop-off points, seat numbers and captured total fare.
- Continue an unpaid booking (PENDING) straight to payment from the list.

---

## P7 — Notifications

**Bullet:**
> Departure changes and payment updates where you can see them — no more guesswork at the gate.

- Payment confirmations, booking updates and cancellation alerts land in the Notifications inbox.
- Mark read / track status per message: Email/SMS push icons clearly labelled.
- Day-of-trip reminders keep riders on time without the overload.

---

## P8 — AI trip planner (assistant)

**Bullet:**
> Not sure of the route? Ask VyGo — it plans, searches and books.

- Ask conversational questions: "E01 Colombo to Matara", "Which bus goes to Kataragama?" or "Expressway or normal?"
- Assistant replies with real trip suggestions and one-tap links into the search results.
- Fallback for empty search: one "Ask VyGo Assistant" button rescues the dead end.

---

## P9 — Operator- & admin-facing (business selling point)

**Bullet:**
> The same platform powers drivers, admins and transport accountants.

> For operators: dashboard with fleet list, today's occupancy and 7-day revenue; buses, schedules
and reports in one console.
> For admins: operator approval flow, complaint desk, analytics.
> For conductors: QR ticket validation console (`/conductor`) to verify passes at the stop.

---

## P10 — Local-first

**Bullet:**
> Built in Sri Lanka for Sri Lankan roads — tarmac to temple town.

> Covers expressway corridors AND the classic government SLTB numbered services that run daily.
> Government (SLTB) and private operators are transparently labelled — pick either side with confidence.
> LKR fare labels, local city names and boarding points match how riders actually talk.
